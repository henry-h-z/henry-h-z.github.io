--O-  Articulated Fish  -O--

Manufactured from 6061 aluminum on a Haas Mini Mill using fixture plate
Head subdivision modelled in Blender, bones and fins modelled parametrically in solidworks
3D CAM designed in Mastercam with appropriate feeds and speeds
Machined in 2 halves, pinned and glued together, and finished with sanding and bead-blasting
